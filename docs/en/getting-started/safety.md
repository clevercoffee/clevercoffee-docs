# Electrical safety

!!! danger "230 V mains voltage can kill"
    Espresso machines contain hazardous mains-voltage circuits. A software
    guide cannot make an unsafe electrical installation safe.

- Disconnect the machine from the mains before opening it or touching any
  internal wiring.
- Do not work on, test, or troubleshoot exposed mains-voltage circuits while
  the machine is energized.
- Follow the machine manufacturer's service instructions and the applicable
  electrical regulations.
- If you are not qualified to modify mains-powered equipment, stop and ask a
  qualified professional to do the work.
- Keep low-voltage controller wiring separate from mains-voltage wiring and
  use suitable insulation, strain relief, enclosures, and protective
  earthing as required by the design and local regulations.
- Never rely on this documentation as a wiring diagram. Use the instructions
  for the exact board and machine revision being built.

Flashing firmware through USB does not remove the electrical hazards of the
machine installation. Disconnect the espresso machine from mains power before
installing or servicing the controller.
